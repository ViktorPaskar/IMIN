FAQ

Still overthinking?

Didn’t find it? contact@imin.support or DM us on Instagram. A person answers.

How does it work?

Pick a night. Make an account. Tap I’m in. Show your QR at the door.

Can I come alone?

Yes. That’s kind of the point.

How much is it?

Depends on the night. The price is on each night’s page, and any fee is shown before you pay.

Is this a dating thing?

No. It’s a night out.

Why do I need an account?

One place for all your nights and tickets. It’s also where we’re building the “someone to go with” part.

Do I have to drink?

No. What’s in your cup is your business.

How old do I need to be?

18+. You may be asked for ID at the door.

Will I be in photos?

Probably. We film and take photos at our nights, and there’s a sign at the door. See a photo of you that you want gone? Message us and we’ll take it down.

What do you do with my data?

We use it to get you in the door. News only if you tick the box. Privacy

Who’s behind IMIN?

Four of us: Bohdan, Ivan, Violeta and Viktor. Say hi: contact@imin.support
